Two ways to divide a region now: mullion/transom — a real frame member, needed wherever a plain "Fixed" panel (no sash of its own) sits, since it has nothing else to butt against — versus add sash, which carves off a sash (horizontal slider or a non-operable fixed pane) that overlaps its neighbour by an interlock amount you set, instead of sitting either side of a physical bar. The overlap is shown as a dashed line, not a solid member.

Simplification worth knowing: the tool doesn't yet enforce that the far side of an overlap must also be a sash — you could still mark that remainder a plain "Fixed" pane, which wouldn't be physically correct against an interlocking neighbour. Also not yet built: editing a sash's own size or overlap after it's placed (only removing it and re-adding is possible for now).

Head, sill and each jamb are independent, editable dimensions. The overall width/height you enter are the outer frame's own outside dimensions; the opening is derived by subtracting the frame members (openingW = overallW − jambL − jambR, openingH = overallH − head − sill). With no frame added, the main origin is the opening's own bottom-left corner; with a frame added, it's the true outside corner of the frame. Each pane's dotted outer edge is the sash's true outer edge, hidden inside whatever it's mounted to; the solid orange band inside is its own visible frame face, editable per pane (fixed panes have no band of their own). Double-click any dimension value on the drawing to edit it.
